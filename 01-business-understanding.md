\# Business Understanding





\## Project Overview





This project focuses on redesigning the Rafters Interio website as part of the Future Interns UI/UX Internship. The goal is to analyze the existing user experience, identify usability issues, and design a user-centered solution that improves usability, trust, and engagement.





\## Business Objective





\- Increase consultation inquiries.

\- Build trust and credibility with potential clients.

\- Showcase completed interior design projects effectively.

\- Improve the overall user experience.

\- Strengthen the company's online presence.





\## Problem Statement





Although the current website has a clean and modern appearance, it does not clearly communicate the company's value proposition. Users may find it difficult to quickly understand why they should choose Rafters Interio, explore completed projects, and confidently proceed to book a consultation.





\## Project Goals





\- Understand the business and user needs.

\- Conduct UX research and usability evaluation.

\- Identify pain points in the existing experience.

\- Create research-driven design solutions.

\- Design an intuitive and user-friendly interface.





\## Expected Deliverables





\- Business Understanding

\- Competitor Analysis

\- Website Audit

\- Nielsen's Heuristic Evaluation

\- Research Synthesis

\- Affinity Mapping

\- User Personas

\- Empathy Maps

\- How Might We (HMW) Statements

\- Information Architecture

\- User Flow

\- Low-Fidelity Wireframes

\- High-Fidelity UI Design

\- Interactive Prototype

\- UX Case Study





\## Outcome





A clear understanding of the business goals, user needs, and project scope was established. This foundation guided the research phase and ensured that every design decision aligned with both user expectations and business objectives.





\## Next Step





Conduct a competitor analysis to understand industry standards, identify best practices, and uncover opportunities for improving the user experience.
