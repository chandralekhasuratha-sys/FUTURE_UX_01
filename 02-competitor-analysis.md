\# Competitor Analysis



\## Purpose



The competitor analysis was conducted to understand how competing interior design companies present their services, showcase their projects, and build trust with potential clients. The insights gathered helped identify opportunities for improving the Rafters Interio website.



\## Competitors Analyzed



\- iCraft Designz

\- Faithwood

\- SHA Interiors



\## Analysis Summary



\### iCraft Designz



Strengths

\- Modern visual design

\- Strong portfolio presentation

\- Clear service categories



Weaknesses

\- Limited explanation of the design process

\- Consultation journey could be more visible



\### Faithwood



Strengths

\- Clean navigation

\- Well-organized project gallery

\- Professional branding



Weaknesses

\- Homepage contains a large amount of information

\- Important CTAs are less noticeable



\### SHA Interiors



Strengths

\- Attractive project showcase

\- Trust-building through completed projects

\- Consistent visual identity



Weaknesses

\- Some pages require multiple clicks

\- Limited explanation of services



\## Opportunities for Rafters Interio



\- Create a stronger first impression.

\- Improve trust through testimonials and completed projects.

\- Simplify navigation.

\- Highlight the consultation process.

\- Make the portfolio easier to explore.
